## Transcribe 1.7.1

A quality release. Transcriptions now go through a cleanup stage before they reach your cursor, custom vocabulary finally works everywhere (and stops leaking), and your clipboard survives a dictation.

### ✨ New

- **Cleaner transcriptions.** When the recognizer hears silence or background noise it has a habit of inventing sign-off phrases — a run of "Thank you." or "Thanks for watching!" that you never said. Those are now removed before the text is pasted. They're only dropped when they're a whole sentence on their own, so a genuine "Thank you for the update." is never touched.
- **Word corrections.** Settings → General → **Corrections** now has a *Heard → Replace with* table. If dictation consistently gets a name or product wrong, fix it once and it's fixed every time — on every backend, including ones that don't support vocabulary hints.
- **Custom vocabulary now works on cloud transcription.** Previously it only reached the local model and was silently ignored the moment you switched to Google or Transcribe Pro cloud. It now reaches the recognizer *and* is used by the AI as the correct spelling for your names and terms.
- **Your clipboard is restored.** Dictating used to overwrite whatever you had copied. Now the previous contents come back after the text is pasted.
- Optional **filler-word removal** (um, uh, you know) — off by default, since it edits your actual words.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Custom vocabulary no longer leaks into your transcripts.** The biggest fix in this release: your glossary terms could appear in the output as if you'd spoken them — most often after a pause or over background noise. That's a quirk of how the recognizer uses vocabulary as context, and it's now detected and removed.
- **Scrolling no longer changes your settings.** Rolling the mouse wheel over a dropdown while scrolling a settings page used to silently change the value underneath the cursor — including your model or language. The page now scrolls instead.
- **Smart Actions work on Anthropic.** The Anthropic engine was sending an internal example instead of what you actually dictated, so it responded to the wrong text entirely.
- The Corrections table no longer clips what you're typing.

### Notes

- Nothing is enabled that changes your words without asking: cleanup removes only phantom text, never rewrites your phrasing, capitalization or punctuation. Filler removal is opt-in, and the whole pass can be switched off in Settings → General → Corrections.
- Your own word corrections keep working even with that switch off — they're a deliberate choice, not a guess.
