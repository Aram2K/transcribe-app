## Transcribe 1.6.1 — fit-your-screen update

A fast follow-up to 1.6.0 focused on how the app feels on *your* display.

### 🪟 Windows that fit your screen

- **Every window now sizes itself proportionally to your display** — a
  comfortable panel on a big monitor, a compact one on a scaled laptop. No
  more towers that overflow the screen or title bars opening above the top
  edge.
- **Every window is now freely resizable** — drag any edge or the new corner
  grip. The app remembers not to fight your chosen position; it only nudges a
  window back if part of it would end up off-screen.
- First-run onboarding no longer has a fixed size that overflowed small
  displays.

### 🖱️ Tray that responds

- **One click on the tray icon opens the app** — single, double, or middle
  click all work, and the window now reliably comes to the front even when it
  was minimized (the "clicked many times, nothing happened" bug).
- The right-click menu starts with a bold **Open Settings** as its first item.

### 🧭 Smoother first run

- Finishing onboarding ("Continue as guest" or signing in) now opens the app
  panel instead of silently minimizing to the tray.

### 🛠 Fixes

- Pro dialog: plan prices no longer get clipped at the bottom.
- AI Actions: the rule-based formatter is no longer a pickable engine (it
  remains the app's invisible safety fallback). The local Qwen/Gemma engine
  takes its place everywhere.
