## Transcribe 1.9.3

Faster dictation, a Live Assistance that hears both sides of the call and knows what it's about, images in your questions, mixed-language transcription, and Mac fixes.

### ⚡ Faster

- **Alt+R is quicker.** The speech model loads when the app starts, so your first dictation doesn't wait for it. If you've stopped talking when you press Enter, your text comes about half a second sooner, and it's pasted the moment your keys are up.
- **Esc is instant.** The pill disappears the moment you press it.

### 🪟 Live Assistance

- **Hears both sides.** Start now records your microphone and your computer's sound, whatever Record Meeting is set to. Before, "System sound only" left your own voice out.
- **Session context.** Click **+ Add context** before or during a call and write what it's about: who you are, who you're talking to, what you want from it. It's locked in for the session, and every answer uses it.
- **Images.** Snip part of your screen with the new button next to Send, or paste an image with Ctrl+V (⌘V). It goes with your next question. Send it with nothing typed to get an answer about the image. On Windows the snipping screen is hidden from screen sharing.
- **Shorter, simpler answers.**
- **Sent means sent.** The question box confirms your question went and clears itself.

### 🗣 Mixed languages

- **New "Mixed languages" option** in Settings → Spoken language. Tick the languages you switch between, even mid-sentence, and every word stays in the language and alphabet you spoke it in. Works best with Pro cloud or a Gemini key.
- **Foreign words stay as spoken.** With one language set, English words in an Armenian sentence (for example) are no longer translated.

### 🍎 Mac

- **Hotkeys fixed.** The hotkey picker shows Mac keys (⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧), ⌘Q or ⌘W no longer quit or close the window while you pick, and it no longer closes the app.
- **Private, honestly.** On macOS 14 and earlier, Live Assistance is now hidden from screen sharing. macOS 15 and later don't let apps hide a window from screen sharing, so there the badge says **Not hidden**. Share a single window instead of your whole screen to keep the card out.
