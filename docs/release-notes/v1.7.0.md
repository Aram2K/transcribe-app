## Transcribe 1.7.0

The biggest meeting update yet — your meetings now tell you **who said what**, summarize themselves **as you talk**, and let you pick your models right in the record window. Plus a batch of fixes to dictation and meeting reliability.

### ✨ New

- **Speaker identification (beta).** When you stop a meeting, Transcribe now labels the transcript by speaker — *Speaker 1, Speaker 2, …* — so you can follow who said what. It runs **fully locally** on your machine (no audio leaves your device for this), using an open-source diarization model that downloads once on first use (~40 MB).
- **Live meeting summary.** The record window now shows a running summary that builds itself every few seconds during the conversation, instead of an empty recording box. Your raw transcript and your own notes sit right beneath it.
- **Pick your models in the meeting window.** New dropdowns let you choose the **transcription model** (Whisper size) and the **summary & notes AI** (a fast local rule-based engine, a local LLM, your own cloud key, or Transcribe Pro's managed cloud) — without digging into Settings.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Dictation no longer gets stuck on "Finalising…"** on machines with an NVIDIA GPU but missing CUDA libraries — it now reliably falls back to the CPU instead of hanging.
- **Fewer hallucinations.** Whisper no longer fills silence with phantom "Thank you." / "I'll see you next time." lines during meetings.
- **Your meeting is never lost.** If the AI summary fails, your full transcript stays on screen (and on disk) with a one-click **Retry Summary** — no more losing a recording to a summary error.
- **Pro accounts no longer get asked for an API key** when generating meeting notes — Pro now correctly uses the managed cloud, and any setup gap falls back to a local summary instead of erroring.
- **Cleaner live transcript** — flowing sentences instead of chopped, timestamp-prefixed fragments, and the record header now shows exactly what's being captured (mic, system audio, or both).
- The "Generating notes" step no longer makes the window look frozen — it shows live progress (transcribing → identifying speakers → writing summary).

### Notes

- Speaker identification is labelled **beta**: accuracy depends on clear, separated voices, and very long meetings take a little longer to process after you hit Stop (it runs on the CPU so it never slows your transcription).
