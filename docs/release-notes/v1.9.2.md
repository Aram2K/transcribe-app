## Transcribe 1.9.2

A redesigned dictation pill: a small liquid-glass capsule you can drag anywhere, with a recording dot that makes it obvious you're live. And **Manage subscription** now works.

### 🎙 Dictation pill

- **Smaller and clearer.** The white card is now a compact glass pill at the bottom-centre of the screen you're typing on. There's no "Recording" label any more: a red dot with a pulsing ring shows you're live, the bars follow your voice, and a timer ticks.
- **Glass that fits in**: light glass over bright apps, dark glass over dark ones, showing what's behind it live. To keep one look, set `overlay_theme` to `"dark"` or `"light"` in config.json.
- **Drag it anywhere.** It remembers the spot. Double-click it to send it back to the bottom-centre.
- **Never steals focus**: clicking or dragging it keeps your cursor in the app you're typing in, and clicks next to it go straight through.
- **Hidden from screen shares and recordings** by default, like Live Assistance. To show it in your own screen recordings, set `overlay_private` to `false` in config.json; it then uses a solid smoked look.
- **Hints only while you learn**: the Enter and Esc keycaps show briefly on your first five dictations after each app start, then the timer takes their place.
- **Every stage in the same pill**: transcribing (with your words streaming in, and "still working…" if it takes a while), thinking, pasted, and errors with a clear next step. "Pasted" and errors stay up while your pointer is on them.
- **Lighter**: it uses about half the CPU of the old overlay while you record.
- **Follows your Windows settings**: its animations stop when Animation effects are off, and it turns solid when Transparency effects are off.

### 💳 Subscription

- **Manage subscription works.** Settings → Account → Manage subscription (also in the tray menu) opens Stripe's portal already signed in. There you can cancel (cancel during the free trial and you're never charged), change your card or download invoices.
- **Fair use, stated plainly**: Pro's unlimited Smart Actions have a fair-use limit of 1,000 requests a day, shared with Live Assistance. If you ever reach it, the message tells you when it resets.
