## Transcribe 1.6.12 — meetings that work, an app that opens

### 🎙️ Meeting recording, fixed end to end

- **No more crash on stop.** Stopping a meeting could kill the whole app: the
  audio stream was closed while a read was still blocked on a silent loopback
  device. The recording thread now owns its streams and shuts them down safely.
- **The right system audio is captured.** Loopback matching could grab a
  virtual device (e.g. Steam's "Speakers (Steam Streaming Microphone)") instead
  of your real speakers — recording silence. The default output's true loopback
  twin is now resolved natively.
- **Live transcription actually streams.** Your microphone is transcribed live
  even when nothing is playing on the computer; system audio is mixed in as it
  arrives.
- **Meeting notes land in History** (with the full transcript), respecting the
  history toggle and Privacy Mode.
- **Language selector in the meeting window** — defaults to your app-wide
  language, applies only to that meeting.
- Starting a meeting from Settings no longer closes the Settings window.

### 🪟 The app behaves like an app

- **Opening Transcribe opens the panel.** No more silent start to the tray
  where it looked like nothing happened — including right after an install or
  update. (The Windows-startup entry still launches quietly in the background.)
- **No more scary terminal flash.** A hidden system probe popped a PowerShell
  window over the app on open; it now runs invisibly.
- **Minimize and maximize buttons** on the Settings, Meeting, and History
  windows.

### ✂️ Dictation endings

- The end-of-recording cutoff is fixed at both root causes: a thread race that
  could drop the final audio frames, and a post-roll that was too short for
  words spoken right into the hotkey press (now ~half a second).

### 🔁 Predictable default

- Every fresh app start begins in **transcribe-only** mode; Smart Actions is a
  per-session choice you re-enable when you want it.
