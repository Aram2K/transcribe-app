## Transcribe 1.9.1

**Live Assistance** (formerly Live Prompter) becomes a real-time companion that answers for you mid-call, on a much faster AI. This release also brings sturdier meetings and sign-in, and a round of fixes across Settings, History and your account.

### 🪟 Live Assistance

- **Renamed** from Live Prompter and rebuilt as a companion: it answers and solves, it doesn't summarise.
- **Answers on its own** the moment someone asks you something, while the card is open. Turn this on or off with **Answer automatically when someone asks a question** in Settings.
- **Answer**, **Follow-ups**, **Solve screen** or a typed question (new send button) work any time. An automatic answer never cuts off one you asked for.
- **Faster Pro answers**: Pro now runs on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with thinking switched off, so first words typically appear in under 2 seconds. If it is slow or unavailable, Gemini Flash (then Mistral) answers automatically.
- **Screen context that works**: with **Screen** on, every answer can use the monitor under your cursor as context. The old keyword guessing missed questions like "how do I solve this?".
- **Clearer, safer answers**: Live Assistance is told the transcript may be imperfect and unlabelled, never invents facts about you, and never pretends to see a screen it wasn't shown.
- **Always the Pro cloud for Pro users**, even if a small offline model is set for dictation. In Privacy Mode it stays on your device and never takes screenshots.
- **Faster live transcription**: shorter chunks, so questions are caught sooner.
- **Follow-ups remember** your last two answers ("and the second part?").
- **The recording is saved** with the session. Download it from **History → Download Audio**.
- **Visible mode**: a darker glass with no rectangle border.
- **Tells you when it can't see the screen**, e.g. an engine that can't read images or Privacy Mode, instead of guessing.
- **Keeps a partial answer** if the connection drops mid-answer.

### 🗓 Meetings

- **One failed piece of audio no longer loses the whole meeting.** The notes are made from everything that was transcribed, and the recording always stays in History.
- **Transcripts are no longer duplicated.**
- **Meetings over 2 hours keep their full transcript and notes.** The saved recording ends at about 2 hours, and the app tells you.
- **Starting a session no longer changes the Whisper model or AI engine** you chose in Settings.
- **A new session never reuses the previous meeting's title, attendees or folder.**

### ✍️ Dictation

- **Custom vocabulary** only fixes how your own terms are spelled. It no longer:
  - inserts terms nobody said;
  - changes the capitalisation of ordinary words ("will", "It");
  - changes word endings (Екатерине, Швейцарии, "transcribed");

  and it is much faster with large vocabularies.
- **Pro cloud dictation while offline** now falls back to your local model instead of failing.

### ⚙️ Settings, History and tray

- **API keys** now sit inside each model's card.
- **The Settings window** opens wide enough to show every tab.
- **Clear History** now really clears everything, meetings included. **Cancel** is the default button, and it tells you exactly what was deleted.
- **Export CSV / TXT** in Settings → History works again.
- **Transcribe Files** is in the tray menu.
- **Updates**: after downloading, the app closes properly so the installer can finish, and it never offers the version you already have.
- **Launching the app again** brings up the running copy instead of doing nothing.

### 👤 Account & Pro

- **Pro unlocks right after checkout**, with no restart needed.
- **Your session and engine choice survive glitches**: a Supabase hiccup no longer signs you out, and starting offline no longer hides your Pro engine or your own API keys. Both come back as soon as you're online.
- **Retrying Google sign-in** right after closing the tab now works.

### 🔒 Privacy & reliability

- **Anonymous usage analytics fixed**: no lost events, correct timestamps, no duplicates, one-year retention, and never an email address.
- **Long meeting notes** from Pro are no longer cut off after 30 seconds.

### Notes

- Live Assistance is a privacy feature for your own notes during calls. Recording-consent guidance still applies, and it is not for interviews, exams or assessments where assistance is prohibited.
- Live Assistance transcribes and answers in English by default.
- macOS: the card is not excluded from screen capture, because Apple provides no public API for it since 15.4. The badge shows this.
