## Transcribe 1.9.4

Opens faster, uses less memory, starts with your computer, and dictation is never limited.

### ⚡ Faster and lighter

- **Opens faster.** The app does a few seconds less work every time it starts.
- **Less memory when you're not using it.** The speech model is freed after 10 idle minutes, and a downloaded AI model after 5. It comes back the next time you need it.
- **Optional NVIDIA speed-up.** On a PC with an NVIDIA graphics card, the app can download NVIDIA's GPU library once (528 MB) for much faster transcription. It's offered in Settings, never forced.

### 🎙 No limits

- **Dictation is never limited**, with or without an account. It runs on your own computer.
- **Files up to 5 hours, for everyone.**

### 🖥 Everyday

- **Start at login.** A switch in Settings opens the app when you sign in to your computer. It respects Windows' own Startup apps setting.
- **Live Assistance snips stay private.** You crop the screenshot inside the card, so nothing new appears on a shared screen.
- **On a Mac,** the privacy badge says "May be hidden": macOS doesn't let any app promise its window is hidden from every screen share.
- **Language lists** start with English, then go alphabetically.
